About GAURD

About GAURD

GAURD — Geographic Urban Analysis of Risk Dynamics.

Compare reported crime, national rates, and four-year forecasts to help research your next move.

Data sources

Sources and methods

Crime rates
Annual offenses per 100,000 people. National and reported city rates share the same year and category.
Forecasts
LightGBM models project four years from the reporting year. Validation metrics are in each chart’s model details.
Coverage
Citywide rates may cover different areas from city boundaries. Local conditions vary.
City locations
2025 Census Gazetteer representative points. Catalog populations are reference values.
Regional boundaries
Natural Earth, public domain. Simplified administrative boundaries.
Background map
OpenFreeMap vector tiles, © OpenMapTiles, using © OpenStreetMap contributors. A bundled Natural Earth map is available if tiles cannot load. The locally customized Positron style is by OpenMapTiles contributors, derived from CartoDB Basemaps by Stamen and Paul Norman. Style attribution and license.
News & guide
Gemma researches recent coverage and cites its sources. News context does not change the model forecast.
